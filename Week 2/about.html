About Retro Game Co.

Our Story

Retro Game Co. started with a love for the N64, PS1, and PS2 games we grew up with. Buying retro games online can be a gamble, so every game we sell is tested, cleaned, and given an honest condition grade before it is listed.

Condition Grading Guide

How we grade every game
Grade What It Means
Excellent Looks almost new. Clean label or disc with little to no wear.
Good Light wear such as small scratches or minor label scuffs. Plays perfectly.
Fair Noticeable wear, torn label, or heavier scratches. Tested and fully working.

Loose means game only. CIB (complete in box) means the game comes with its original case and manual.
